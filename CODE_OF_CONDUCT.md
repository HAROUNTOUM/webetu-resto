# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never ask for or publish someone's matricule, password, JWT,
  Telegram token, screenshots of their account or other personal data.
- Keep discussions on topic. This project is about automating one's own meal
  reservations within the rules of the service.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory jokes or sexualized attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging account sharing, credential theft, quota bypass or abuse of the
  university service.
- Spam, sustained disruption or bad-faith arguments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban contributors who violate
this code. Reports can be sent privately to the maintainer listed in
`pyproject.toml`; they will be handled confidentially.
